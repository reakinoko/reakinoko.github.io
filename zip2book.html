Zip2Book for Android

Local archive bookshelf for Android

Overview

Zip2Book imports ZIP, RAR, TAR, and related image archives into a local bookshelf. It is designed for readers who want to keep manga or comic archives organized on their Android device without uploading their files to a separate service.

Features

The app supports local archive import, cover selection, reading progress, page-by-page or vertical reading, left-to-right or right-to-left page direction, landscape spreads, page notes, and duplicate import detection.
